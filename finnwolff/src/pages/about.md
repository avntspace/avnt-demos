## Building a future that we can all be excited about

Finn Wolff started his journey as an entrepreneur at just five years old when he started selling candy & sodas to hungry customers at the back of an old barn at his grandparents' Christmas tree farm in Northern Virginia. This event planted the seed not only for the growth of his first business, Finn's Snack Shack, but it also planted the seeds for a future of building businesses and solving problems.

Finn found a passion for technology after being inspired by the launch of the iPhone and the opportunities that created to communicate, create content, and build apps that change the world. Finn began his first business in the tech space by purchasing old iPhones, gaming consoles and electronics from his friends and people at his school and re-selling them on eBay for a profit. Finn operated this business throughout high school until getting inspired by the start-up boom in Silicon Valley and striving to create an internet company that could have a larger impact.

While Finn wanted to start a tech company, he knew he'd need to build a website to do so. However, with little money to invest in hiring a developer, in 2016 Finn decided to teach himself to code and build websites himself. Soon after learning to build websites, Finn taught himself to code Swift in order to build iOS apps and started working on his first tech company in the fashion and creative space.

While attending UC Berkeley, Finn started a number of companies that included Schmooze, Xalted, companies in the blockchain space, and others focused around solving real world problems he noticed both at his university and within his passions.

Finn took a leap of faith after dropping out of the University of California, Berkeley in 2019, where he started his first of many fast-growing tech companies full time that help disrupt and grow the next generation of fashion, art, & culture.

In addition to his entrepreneurial endeavors, Finn has also capitalized on his passion for fashion, technology and culture with investments in multiple companies in the technology space, streetwear, Web3, and more.

Finn has also started philanthropic endeavors after the passing of one of his best friends in late 2020, with initiatives that center around providing relief for families in need, criminal justice reform, positive news organizations, and more.

Finn continues to build new companies at the cutting edge of technology that serve to push the culture forward, inspire humanity to get excited about a brighter future, and change people's lives around the world for the better.

Outside of the business world, Finn enjoys being present with his family & friends skiing, surfing, traveling, meeting new people, going on adventures, and making memories that'll last a lifetime.
